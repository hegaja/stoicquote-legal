Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

"The Daily Stoic Quotes" ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial automation tool built and operated by a single individual developer, used only to publish that developer's own video content to their own social media accounts.

1. Who this applies to

The App has no public users, no sign-up, and no audience-facing accounts of its own. The only individual whose data the App ever touches is its developer.

2. What data the App handles

The App stores, on the developer's own private machine, OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by TikTok (and other platforms) that authorize it to publish video content to the developer's own account. It does not collect, store, process, or share personal data belonging to any other individual, and does not use any data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

3. Data sharing

Data is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. The only external services contacted are the official APIs of the platforms the developer chooses to publish to (e.g. TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), for the sole purpose of uploading the developer's own content.

4. Data retention

Authorization tokens are retained locally for as long as the developer continues to use the App, and can be revoked at any time via the relevant platform's account settings (e.g. TikTok's connected-apps page).

5. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time as the App's functionality changes.

Questions about this document can be sent to the developer via the contact details on file with the relevant platform's developer program.
